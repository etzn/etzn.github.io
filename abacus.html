The complement, by hand

Why nine is IX, not VIIII

When you add 9 to most numbers, there aren’t enough beads to add 5 and then add 4. Instead, you have to get there by adding 10 and then subtracting 1. So that’s how the Romans write it down! Try it out yourself.
