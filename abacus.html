►Why nine is IX, not VIIII

When you add nine to most numbers, there aren’t enough beads to add five and then add four: try it out yourself below. Instead, you have to get there by adding ten and then subtracting one. That’s how the Romans wrote nine: “one before ten”, or IX!

Four is IV rather than IIII for the same reason. Can you prove it to yourself?
