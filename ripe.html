Serving tips by age

Tips follow the standard choking guidance in the CDC’s Choking Hazards guide for infants and toddlers and the California Department of Public Health’s Choking Prevention for Young Children. Pick your child's age under Settings. This is general guidance, not medical advice, and your pediatrician knows your child best.

How good are these dates?

They are typical harvest windows for northern Illinois, southwest Michigan and southern Wisconsin, which is what Chicago markets sell. Weather moves them a week or two either way, and peaks are fuzzier than starts and ends. The market table is the final word.

Winter

From about December to April almost nothing grows outdoors here. "From the cellar" means local crops kept in cold storage. "Shipped in" lists produce grown elsewhere during its own peak, when grocery quality and price are best.

About the plates

Every plate comes from two public-domain collections: fruit from the USDA Pomological Watercolor Collection (1886–1942), painted by Deborah Passmore, Amanda Newton, Royal Steadman and their colleagues; vegetables from the engravings in Vilmorin-Andrieux’s Les plantes potagères (Paris, 1883), tinted here; and a few stragglers from Francisco Manuel Blanco’s Flora de Filipinas (1880s) and Descourtilz’s Flore médicale des Antilles (1827).
