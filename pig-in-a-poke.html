PIG IN A POKE

wherein the cat must not get out of the bag

Tap a pawing sack to stuff the cat back in. Never strike a pig.

Market Day, 1387

Your pokes hold “pigs.” Most hold cats. Buyers pay well — until a cat gets out of the bag.

Tap a cat’s paw to stuff it back in and bank the sale.
Never tap a snout — an oink of protest draws the crowd.

Three scandals and the mob runs you out of town.

The Cat Is Out of the Bag!
